## It starts with belonging

I am Sourashtrian. Those words hold more than a name: they carry language, family, memory, knowledge, and the many ways our people have made a home across generations.

I started this community space because the things we know are worth protecting—and sharing. Every story remembered, skill taught, question answered, or thoughtful idea offered gives the next person another place to begin.

## Protect, develop, strengthen

**Protect** the language, traditions, family histories, and knowledge that matter to our community. Listen carefully, record them with permission, and respect the different ways people live their Sourashtrian identity.

**Develop** what we can build together: useful resources, creative work, community initiatives, and digital tools that make knowledge easier to discover and share.

**Strengthen** the connections between our people. When someone has a question or a useful experience to pass on, a supportive community can help them find their way forward.

## A living community

This is an invitation, not a finished record. Bring what you know, what you are learning, what you are working on, or the question you have been carrying. We can preserve what matters and make room for what comes next—together.
